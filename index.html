What is bún chả?

Hanoi's favourite lunch

Bún chả (pronounced “boon chah”) is a grilled-pork noodle dish from Hà Nội, the capital of Vietnam. Bún means rice vermicelli and chả means grilled meat.

Pork patties and slices of marinated pork belly are cooked over charcoal until smoky and caramelised, then dropped into a bowl of nước chấm – a warm, light broth of fish sauce, vinegar, sugar, garlic and chilli, with slivers of pickled green papaya and carrot.

It's served alongside a plate of cool rice noodles and a basket of fresh herbs – lettuce, perilla, mint and Vietnamese balm. Dip, wrap and slurp as you like. Add a few crispy nem rán spring rolls and you've got a Hanoi feast.

  1. 1

    Grill

    Pork patties & belly, marinated and charred over coals.

  2. 2

    Dip

    Pick up noodles and dunk them into the warm broth with the pork.

  3. 3

    Herb

    Tear in fresh herbs, add garlic and chilli to taste.

  4. 4

    Repeat

    Keep going until the bowl is empty. Spring rolls encouraged.
