What is
“bún chả”?

Say it “boon chah”

“Bún”rice vermicelli

“Chả”pork grilled over charcoal

Bún is rice vermicelli, and Chả is meat grilled over charcoal. In Hanoi you dip the cold noodles and fresh herbs into a warm bowl of light, sweet and sour fish sauce where the grilled pork sits, so each mouthful is smoky and fresh at once.

It is the street food that stands for the North of Vietnam, the way phở and bánh mì are known everywhere. We cook it the northern way, and bring dishes from Huế, Hội An and the streets of Saigon too.
